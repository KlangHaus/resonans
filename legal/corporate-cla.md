**Corporate Contributor Licence Agreement — Resonans**

This agreement supplements the Individual Contributor Licence Agreement (`cla-1.0`,
"the ICLA"). Words defined there have the same meaning here.

**1. Who signs.** The Company named below, through a person authorised to bind it.

**2. Rights granted.** The Company grants KlangHaus the same rights, on the same
terms, as clauses 2 and 3 of the ICLA, for every Contribution made by a Scheduled
Person. **Clauses 2 and 3 of the ICLA are incorporated into this agreement by
reference and are not repeated here.**

**3. Scheduled Persons.** The persons listed in the Schedule. The Company may add
persons by written notice to KlangHaus; removing a person does not affect
Contributions already made.

**4. The Company's assurance.** The Company confirms that it owns or otherwise
controls the rights in the Scheduled Persons' Contributions, and that it may grant the
rights in clause 2. **If that turns out not to be so for a given Contribution, the
Company will tell KlangHaus without undue delay.**

**5. Moral rights.** Clause 4 of the ICLA applies: nothing here asks any individual to
waive moral rights, and naming in version history satisfies section 3(1) of the Danish
Copyright Act.

**6. The public record.** Clause 8 of the ICLA applies, with one difference: the
record names **the Company** and the signing date, and the Scheduled Persons appear in
the signature file by their version-control identity only.

**7. No obligation, no warranty, governing law.** Clauses 6, 7 and 9 of the ICLA
apply.

**Signed:** company name · CVR or other company number · signatory's name and role ·
date · version of this agreement. **Schedule:** the Scheduled Persons' version-control
identities.
